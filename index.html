The numbers are measured, not invented

REACH

Max Feret diameter of the sprite's silhouette in source pixels — the longest distance between any two opaque pixels.

MASS

The count of opaque pixels in the icon. Heavier-looking weapons literally have more ink in them.

DMG

round(60 × len/70.58 + 60 × mass/2182) — the pack's real maximum reach (70.58 px) and mass (2182 px) are the only constants.

COLOUR

Impact sparks inherit the sprite's dominant quantised colour, so a violet staff throws violet light.

Assets: PixelSRPG-Forge weapon icons and monster sheets (MIT-licensed repository; the sprites are MapleStory-style game art, used here as a non-commercial demo). 727 of the 728 numbered icons are weapons — #392 is a five-frame "VIP chest" HUD animation and is excluded. All sound is synthesised for this build.
